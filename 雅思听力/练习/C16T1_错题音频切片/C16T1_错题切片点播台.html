🎧 我的跟读录音 00:00
"SARAH: ...they work together to design a special cover that goes round an egg, so that when it's inside they can drop it from a height and it doesn't break."
错因诊断与盲点：你的反馈是“耳朵听到了，手匆忙写成了 age，egg 写成了 age，手脑没有同步”。
1. 耳朵准确抓到了 /eɡ/ 音节，但开局高度紧张，手部运动神经出现乱码投射，误写成了 age；

• ✓ 你做对了 —— 定位词 Stoicism 一出口答案区即到，the most practical of all philosophies 原词稳稳接住。

32 · 从未出版 publication 与 Meditations 书名陷阱

🎧 我的跟读录音 00:00
卷面题干： The Stoics' ideas are surprisingly well known, despite not being intended for [ 32 ]
"LECTURER: Amazingly, we still have access to these ideas, despite the fact that the most famous Stoics never wrote anything down for publication [Q32]. Cato definitely didn't. Marcus Aurelius never intended his Meditations to be anything but personal."
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• ✗ 你的错因：整句让步改写没听懂 + 否定后置误判 —— 你识别出了 famous ⇄ well known 的替换，但 despite the fact that ... never wrote anything down for publication 整句没跟上；等 Cato definitely didn't（加图肯定没有）追加强调时，你以为是否定新信息、答案已过，于是往后找，把书名 Meditations 写成了 meditation。
• 替换改写（附中文）：卷面 ideas are surprisingly well known（思想惊人地广为人知）⇄ 录音 Amazingly, we still have access to these ideas（令人惊讶的是，我们至今仍能接触到这些思想）；卷面 not being intended for ____（并非为____而写）⇄ 录音 never wrote anything down for publication（从未写下任何东西用于出版）——intended for ⇄ wrote down for（打算用于 ⇄ 写下用于）。
• 连词信号：despite the fact that（尽管）= 让步信号，答案在从句末尾；definitely didn't 是省略否定句，didn't 替代的是 wrote anything down for publication——听到省略否定要回看前句，而不是向前找新答案。
• 口诀：让步句（despite）的答案多在句尾；didn't / don't 类省略否定 = 前句复读机，千万别换台；书名（Meditations）、人名永不作填空答案。

"LECTURER: Stoicism had a profound influence on Albert Ellis, who invented Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, which is used to help people manage their problems by changing the way that they think and behave. It's most commonly used to treat depression [Q37]."

• ✗ 你的错因：两题共栖一段，串题抢答 —— Q37/Q38 的答案挤在相邻两句话里。你听到下一句 create our faulty thinking 的 thinking，误以为 treatment for thinking（治疗思维）；其实 thinking 属于 Q38 句，是"要被消除的东西"之一，与 Q37 无关。

• ✗ 你的错因：并列名词堆中抓错 —— 你只听到 symptoms and behaviours 就写了 behave；但 faulty thinking, symptoms and behaviours（错误思维、症状和行为）三个并列名词全是定语从句里"要被挑战消除的东西"，真正的答案在句尾 by using logic instead。
