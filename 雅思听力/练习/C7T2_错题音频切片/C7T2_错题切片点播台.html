LECTURER Things are slightly different in racket sports like tennis. Here the important thing is to have the dominant hand and eye on the one side. This means that there is a bigger area of vision on the side where most of the action occurs. If a player is cross lateral the racket is invisible from the dominant eye for much of the swing. It means that they can only make corrections much later... and often the damage has been done by then.
在网球等球拍类运动中情况略有不同。这里关键是优势手和优势眼在同侧。这意味着在绝大多数动作发生的一侧拥有更大的视野范围。如果选手是交叉侧向型的，在大部分挥拍过程中优势眼都看不到球拍。这意味着他们只能在很久之后才能做出击球动作修正……而那时失误往往已经铸成。
LECTURER And moving to a rather different type of sport which involves large but precise movements - gymnastics. It's been found that cross hand-eye favouring is best. The predominant reason for this is because it aids balance - which is of course absolutely central to performance in this sport.
再转向一种涉及大幅度但极其精准动作的截然不同的运动——体操。研究发现，手眼交叉偏利是最好的。其主要原因是因为它有助于维持身体平衡——而平衡对于这项运动的表现而言当然绝对处于核心地位。
