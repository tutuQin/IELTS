01 · 岗位一所属企业产品 (furniture)

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Administrative assistant in a company that produces [ 1 ] (North London)
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• 改写对应：卷面 produces $\Leftrightarrow$ 录音 design and make；
• 做题心得：Sally 报出公司名称 Home Solutions 之后紧跟核心动词 design and make，顺利锁定不可数名词 furniture，开局稳步拿分。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▼

SALLY: The first one is in a company based in North London. They’re looking for an administrative assistant.

JOE: OK. What sort of company is it?

SALLY: They’re called Home Solutions and they design and make furniture [Q1].

JOE: Oh, I don’t know much about that, but it sounds interesting.

Q6 错题精研 · 35秒切片 ②拼写坑 + ⑦单复数

06 · 仓库助理管理职责 (deliveries)

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Responsibilities: stock management / managing [ 6 ]
⚠️ 错因核心剖析（字母拼写错乱与单复数形态动摇）
• 现场复现：听到了也定位到了，但在落笔拼写时，第二音节元音字母 i 习惯性误写为 e（写成 dele...），并且在单复数上犹豫不决写错；
• 神经认知根因：大脑对词形表征（Orthographic Representation）模糊，delivery 变复数 deliveries 缺乏连贯肌肉记忆；
• 强化训练：大声朗读 /dɪˈlɪvəriz/，连续默写 10 遍 d-e-l-i-v-e-r-i-e-s，杜绝考场手脑脱节。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▲

SALLY: So the second position might suit you better as far as the location goes; that’s for a warehouse assistant and that’s in South London.

JOE: Yes, that would be a lot closer.

SALLY: And you’ve worked in a warehouse before, haven’t you?

JOE: Yes.

SALLY: So as far as the responsibilities for this position go, they want someone who can manage the stock, obviously, and also deliveries [Q6].

JOE: That should be OK. You’ve got to keep track of stuff, but I’ve always been quite good with numbers.

Q7 错题精研 · 29秒切片 ①词义盲区 + ⑥语法脑补

07 · 个人工作素质特征 (tidy)

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Requirements: very organised and [ 7 ]
⚠️ 错因核心剖析（生词盲区与“伪语法前后一致”脑补）
• 现场复现：听出了 tidy 的发音，但因不认识词义，加上空前是 organised，误以为是动词过去分词对称，凭空生造了 tided；
• 神经认知根因：缺乏对基础形容词 tidy（井井有条的）的语义储备，触发了教条的“前后对称语法脑补机制”；
• 防坑铁律：organised 与 tidy 是形容词功能并列（修饰人的性格），绝非字尾拼写对称！凡是听到的声音，原汁原味拼出，绝不可画蛇添足加 -ed。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▲

SALLY: Good, that’s their first requirement. And they want someone who’s computer literate, which we know you are.

JOE: Sure.

SALLY: Then they mention organisational skills. They want someone who’s well organised.

JOE: Yes, I think I am.

SALLY: And tidy [Q7]?

JOE: Yes, they go together really, don’t they?

SALLY: Sure.

Q8 错题精研 · 29秒切片 提前抢跑 + 同义改写脱节

08 · 团队协作习惯 (team)

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Requirements: used to working in a [ 8 ]
⚠️ 错因核心剖析（状语背景词提前抢答与手脑闭塞）
• 现场复现：听到 Joe 说 for the last warehouse job 时，大脑被熟悉的岗位背景词吸引，过早断定答案盲写 workhouse/warehouse，漏掉了后半句真正的主干；
• 同义改写链：卷面 working in a [ 8 ] $\Leftrightarrow$ 录音 being part of a team $\Leftrightarrow$ Sally 确认 used to that；
• 防坑铁律：听到“在上一份工作中（for the last... job）”100% 只是状语背景铺垫！手千万不能动，必须死等主句主干核心名词！
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▲

SALLY: Then the usual stuff; they want someone who can communicate well both orally and in writing.

JOE: OK. And for the last warehouse job I had, one of the things I enjoyed most was being part of a team [Q8]. I found that was really essential for the job.

SALLY: Excellent. Yes, they do mention that they want someone who’s used to that, yes.

JOE: Yes, no problem.

JOE: Right. So what about pay? Can you tell me a bit more about that, please …
