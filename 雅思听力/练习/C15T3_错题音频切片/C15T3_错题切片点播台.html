⚠️ 错因核心剖析（字母拼写错乱与单复数形态动摇）
• 现场复现：听到了也定位到了，但在落笔拼写时，第二音节元音字母 i 习惯性误写为 e（写成 dele...），并且在单复数上犹豫不决写错；
• 神经认知根因：大脑对词形表征（Orthographic Representation）模糊，delivery 变复数 deliveries 缺乏连贯肌肉记忆；
• 强化训练：大声朗读 /dɪˈlɪvəriz/，连续默写 10 遍 d-e-l-i-v-e-r-i-e-s，杜绝考场手脑脱节。

JOE: Yes, that would be a lot closer.

SALLY: And you’ve worked in a warehouse before, haven’t you?

JOE: Yes.

SALLY: So as far as the responsibilities for this position go, they want someone who can manage the stock, obviously, and also deliveries [Q6].

JOE: That should be OK. You’ve got to keep track of stuff, but I’ve always been quite good with numbers.

07 · 个人工作素质特征 (tidy)

准备录音... 00:00

⚠️ 错因核心剖析（生词盲区与“伪语法前后一致”脑补）
• 现场复现：听出了 tidy 的发音，但因不认识词义，加上空前是 organised，误以为是动词过去分词对称，凭空生造了 tided；

JOE: Sure.

SALLY: Then they mention organisational skills. They want someone who’s well organised.

SALLY: And tidy [Q7]?

JOE: Yes, they go together really, don’t they?

SALLY: Sure.

🧼 Early history of keeping clean 清洁的早期历史 · 战报 10 题对 3 错 7

讲座时间轴：史前（Q31 泥垢）→ 古巴比伦（Q32 陶土）→ 古希腊（Q33 金属刮身板）→ 日耳曼高卢（Q34 染发）→ 古罗马（Q35 浴场）→ 中世纪（Q36 瘟疫 / Q37 香水）→ 近代科学（Q38 食盐制碱 / Q39 成为科学 / Q40 重税取消）。
七道错题的三大病根：① 答案前置连环阵（Q33 metal 在生词 strigil 前、Q35 baths 在 aqueducts 前——等定位词=等丢答案）；② 生词卡壳断崖（strigil→unsanitary→连败 Q33-Q38 空窗）；③ 审题与语法自检缺失（Q34 把"染什么"记成"由什么组成"、Q40 把 tax 空耳成 hightext）。Part 4 独白没有对话换气，一题卡壳就是连环崩——本套是最典型的"断崖案例"。
🎨 本篇（Part 4）标注图例 —— 每一题都按同一套颜色标注：
蓝色实线 = 定位词：卷面和录音里同一个词（人名 / 地名 / 年份 / 专有名词），听到它就知道答案区到了；卷面里的定位词和原文里的定位词用同一种蓝，一眼能对上。
黄色虚线 = 替换处：题干和原文换了说法的地方（同义替换 / 词性转换 / 主被动倒置 / 介词框架颠倒），左边题干、右边原文都标黄，成对看就是命题人的改写路径。
绿色 = 答案词；红色删除线 = 干扰项（听到了也要划掉）。
读法：先看题干里蓝色锁定位置 → 再顺着黄色看"卷面怎么变成录音" → 绿色落笔。

33 · 古希腊刮身板：metal 在生词 strigil 之前就说完了

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Ancient Greece: used a strigil – scraper made of [ 33 ] （strigil 是生词定位词；scraper ⇄ 原文 scraped off ... instrument）
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• ✗ 你的错因：定位不到 —— 不是没听到这句，而是答案在定位词前面溜走了：
• 答案前置结构：原文顺序是 scraped off the oil and dirt with a metal instrument known as a strigil —— 答案 metal 先出，2 秒后才出现卷面定位词 strigil。你竖着耳朵等 strigil，等它出现时 metal 已经说完。
• 生词雪上加霜：strigil（刮身板）谁都不认识，但命题人故意把它放在卷面当定位词——听到陌生名词别慌，它只是路标，答案往往在它前面的日常词里（metal 初中词汇）。
• 防坑口诀：卷面结构 "A – B made of ____"（A 是生词时），答案在录音里 A 出现之前 3 秒内。刮痧板是金属做的——语义常识也能兜底。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▼

LECTURER: The early Greeks bathed for aesthetic reasons and apparently didn't use soap. Instead, they cleaned their bodies with blocks of sand, pumice and ashes, then anointed themselves with oil, and scraped off the oil and dirt with a metal [Q33] instrument known as a strigil. They also used oil mixed with ashes. Clothes were washed without soap in streams.

早期希腊人为了美观而沐浴，但显然不用肥皂。他们用砂块、浮石和灰烬擦洗身体，再抹上油，然后用一种叫 strigil（刮身板） 的金属器具刮掉油污。他们还用油混灰烬。衣服则在溪流中不用肥皂直接洗。【Q33 答案句 → metal；答案在生词 strigil 之前出现】

34 · 日耳曼人：肥皂用来染红的是 hair，ashes 是配方

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Ancient Germany and Gaul: used soap to colour their [ 34 ] （colour their ____ ⇄ 原文 tint their ____，要的是身体部位）
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• ✗ 你的错因：听到了 hair 却没写，写了 ashes —— 复盘你自己的话："题目没有审题对，记忆成了由什么组成"。
• 审题失真：卷面写的是 used soap to colour their ____（用来"染"他们的什么），你记成了"肥皂由什么组成"——一个词之差，整个听音目标全错。
• 成分句精准埋雷：原文恰好先说 a substance called soap, made of melted animal fat and ashes（肥皂的成分：动物油脂+灰烬）——带着"组成"的预期去听，ashes 就成了完美诱饵；真正的答案句在后一句：They used this mixture to tint their hair red（用这种混合物把头发染红），tint = colour 改写。
• 防坑口诀：预读时把空格两侧的词都划出来——"colour their + ____" 的坑只能是身体部位/外观词；made of 结构出现时默念"这是配方不是用途"。审题错一道 = 全套目标错位。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▼

LECTURER: The ancient Germans and Gauls are also credited with discovering how to make a substance called soap, made of melted animal fat and ashes. They used this mixture to tint their hair [Q34] red.

古日耳曼人和高卢人也被认为发现了如何制造一种叫“肥皂”的物质——由熔化的动物油脂和灰烬制成（成分干扰：不是 34 题答案）。他们用这种混合物把头发染红。【Q34 答案句 → hair；tint = colour】

35 · 罗马浴场：baths 在定位词 aqueducts 之前就出完了

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： Ancient Rome: from about 312 BC, water carried to Roman [ 35 ] by aqueducts （carried to ... by aqueducts ⇄ 原文 supplied with water from ... aqueducts）
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• ✗ 你的错因：没有听到答案句 —— 又是一道答案前置：
• 结构拆解：卷面 water carried to Roman ____ by aqueducts（水被引水渠送到罗马的____），原文反过来说：The first of the famous Roman baths, supplied with water from their aqueducts, was built around 312 BC —— 先说 baths，插入语里才出现 aqueducts，还把被动（carried to）改成主动（supplied with）。你等 aqueducts 这个定位词，baths 在它前面 2 秒就过完了。
• 两条防线：① 312 BC 是卷面给的数字锚点，它的前后 5 秒就是答案区；② 语义常识——罗马引水渠送水去的地方，除了浴场还能是哪（罗马浴场是常识级考点）。
• 防坑口诀：卷面有数字/年份时，把数字本身当第一定位词；插入语（两个逗号之间的 supplied with...）里藏着定位词时，答案在插入语之前的主干里。
📜 展开中英精听原文与标注文档 ▼

LECTURER: As Roman civilisation advanced, so did bathing. The first of the famous Roman baths [Q35], supplied with water from their aqueducts, was built around 312 BC. The baths were luxurious, and bathing became very popular. And by the second century AD, the Greek physician Galen recommended soap for both medicinal and cleansing purposes.

随着罗马文明进步，洗浴也跟着发展。第一批著名的罗马浴场约建于公元前 312 年，由引水渠供水。浴场奢华，洗浴蔚然成风。到公元 2 世纪，希腊医师盖伦还推荐肥皂兼作药用和清洁。【Q35 答案句 → baths；答案在定位词 aqueducts 之前】

40 · 收官题：high tax 的 tax 就是答案，别写成 hightext

准备录音... 00:00
卷面题干： from 1800s, there was no longer a [ 40 ] on soap （no longer a ____ on soap ⇄ 原文 the high tax was removed）
🎯 考点拆解与定位路径
• ✗ 你的错因：听到了 high tax 却写成 hightext —— 你自己复盘得很准："听到 high tax，没有听懂句意"：
• 句意拆解：Until the 19th century, soap was regarded as a luxury item, and was heavily taxed（19 世纪前肥皂被视为奢侈品，被课以重税）—— tax 第一次以动词 taxed 出现；随后 a development that was reinforced when the high tax was removed（重税取消后这一趋势更加强化）—— tax 第二次以名词出现，正是答案。
• 空耳修复：/tæks/ 被前词 high 的 /aɪ/ 带跑，听成 text 的音。但卷面早就给了答案的语法框架：no longer a ____ on soap —— 冠词 a + 介词 on，坑里必须是单数名词；hightext 根本不是一个英文单词。

直到 19 世纪，肥皂一直被视为奢侈品，在多个国家被课以重税。随着它越来越容易获得，它成了日常必需品——重税取消后这一趋势进一步强化。肥皂从此是普通人买得起的东西，清洁标准随之提升。【Q40 答案句 → tax；taxed 动词先出现，high tax 名词是答案】

LECTURER: Nowadays, we use different products for personal cleanliness, laundry, dishwashing and household cleaning, but this is very much a 20th-century development. The origins of cleanliness date back to prehistoric times. Since water is essential for life, the earliest people lived near water and knew something about its cleansing properties – at least that it rinsed mud [Q31] off their hands.

如今我们把个人清洁、洗衣、洗碗和家居清洁用不同产品分开处理，但这主要是 20 世纪的发展。清洁的起源追溯到史前：水是生命必需，最早的人类临水而居，至少知道水能把手上的泥垢冲掉。【Q31 答案句 → mud】

LECTURER: During the excavation of ancient Babylon, evidence was found that soapmaking was known as early as 2800 BC. Archaeologists discovered cylinders made of clay [Q32], with inscriptions on them saying that fats were boiled with ashes. This is a method of making soap, though there's no reference to the purpose of this material.

古巴比伦的发掘找到了公元前 2800 年就懂得制皂的证据：考古学家发现了陶土圆筒，铭文记载油脂与灰烬同煮——这是一种制皂方法，尽管没提用途。【Q32 答案句 → clay】

LECTURER: The early Greeks bathed for aesthetic reasons and apparently didn't use soap. Instead, they cleaned their bodies with blocks of sand, pumice and ashes, then anointed themselves with oil, and scraped off the oil and dirt with a metal [Q33] instrument known as a strigil. They also used oil mixed with ashes. Clothes were washed without soap in streams.

早期希腊人为美观而沐浴，但显然不用肥皂：用砂块、浮石和灰烬擦身，抹油后再用叫 strigil（刮身板）的金属器具刮掉油污。衣服在溪流中直洗。【Q33 答案句 → metal；答案在生词 strigil 之前】

LECTURER: The ancient Germans and Gauls are also credited with discovering how to make a substance called soap, made of melted animal fat and ashes. They used this mixture to tint their hair [Q34] red.

古日耳曼人和高卢人也被认为发现了制造“肥皂”的方法——由动物油脂和灰烬组成（成分干扰）。他们用这种混合物把头发染红。【Q34 答案句 → hair；tint = colour】

LECTURER: Soap got its name, according to an ancient Roman legend, from Mount Sapo, where animals were sacrificed, leaving deposits of animal fat. Rain washed these deposits, along with wood ashes, down into the clay soil along the River Tiber. Women found that this mixture greatly reduced the effort required to wash their clothes.

据古罗马传说，肥皂得名于萨波山：那里献祭动物留下油脂沉积，雨水把油脂连同木灰冲进台伯河沿岸的黏土里。妇女们发现这种混合物能大大省去洗衣的力气。【过渡段：Soap 名称由来，无考题】

LECTURER: As Roman civilisation advanced, so did bathing. The first of the famous Roman baths [Q35], supplied with water from their aqueducts, was built around 312 BC. The baths were luxurious, and bathing became very popular. And by the second century AD, the Greek physician Galen recommended soap for both medicinal and cleansing purposes.

罗马文明进步，洗浴也随之发展。第一批著名的罗马浴场约建于公元前 312 年，由引水渠供水。到公元 2 世纪，希腊医师盖伦推荐肥皂兼作药用和清洁。【Q35 答案句 → baths；答案在定位词 aqueducts 之前】

LECTURER: After the fall of Rome in 467 AD and the resulting decline in bathing habits, much of Europe felt the impact of filth on public health. This lack of personal cleanliness and related unsanitary living conditions were major factors in the outbreaks of disease [Q36] in the Middle Ages, and especially the Black Death of the 14th century.

公元 467 年罗马陷落后洗浴习惯衰退，欧洲饱受污秽之害。个人清洁缺失加上恶劣卫生条件，是中世纪瘟疫——尤其 14 世纪黑死病——的主要成因。【Q36 答案句 → disease】

LECTURER: Nevertheless, soapmaking became an established craft in Europe, and associations of soapmakers guarded their trade secrets closely. Vegetable and animal oils were used with ashes of plants, along with perfume [Q37], apparently for the first time. Gradually more varieties of soap became available for shaving and shampooing, as well as bathing and laundering.

尽管如此，制皂在欧洲成为成熟行当。人们用植物油、动物油与草木灰一起使用，并首次加入香水。渐渐地，剃须、洗发、沐浴、洗衣用的肥皂种类越来越多。【Q37 答案句 → perfume；plants 只是干扰】

LECTURER: A major step toward large-scale commercial soapmaking occurred in 1791, when a French chemist, Nicolas Leblanc, patented a process for turning salt [Q38] into soda ash, or sodium carbonate. Soda ash is the alkali obtained from ashes that combines with fat to form soap. The Leblanc process yielded quantities of good-quality, inexpensive soda ash.

1791 年制皂商业化迈出重大一步：法国化学家勒布朗取得专利，把食盐转化为纯碱（碳酸钠）——纯碱与油脂结合即成皂。勒布朗法产出了大量优质廉价的纯碱。【Q38 答案句 → salt；turning X into Y = make Y from X】

LECTURER: Modern soapmaking was born some twenty years later, in the early nineteenth century, with the discovery by Michel Eugène Chevreul, another French chemist, of the chemical nature and relationship of fats, glycerine and fatty acids. 约 20 年后的 19 世纪初，现代制皂业诞生：法国化学家谢弗勒尔发现了油脂、甘油和脂肪酸的化学本质与关系，奠定了油脂化学与皂化学的基础，制皂成为一门科学。19 世纪的后续发展让制皂更容易、更便宜。【Q39 答案句 → science】

LECTURER: Until the nineteenth century, soap was regarded as a luxury item, and was heavily taxed in several countries. With this widespread use came the development of milder soaps for bathing and soaps for use in the washing machines that were available to consumers by the turn of the 20th century.

直到 19 世纪，肥皂一直被视为奢侈品，在多国被课以重税。随着越来越易得，它成了日常必需品——重税取消后这一趋势进一步强化，肥皂从此是普通人买得起的，清洁标准随之提升。随着广泛使用，还发展出更温和的沐浴皂和洗衣机专用皂，到 20 世纪初已进入消费者家庭。【Q40 答案句 → tax】
