# 雅思口语 Part 1 · 核心背诵版（Work or Study）

> 💡 **朗读心法**：  
> 1. 斜线 **`/`** 代表**轻微换气与意群自然停顿**（停顿约 0.2~0.3 秒，不要大喘气，重在切分逻辑意群）。  
> 2. 下方每个回答均经过 7.0+ 语块提炼，考场上保持眼神交流、语调自然起伏即可轻松拿分！

---

### 🌟 45 秒超级母题全景（完整流利说 · 附意群停顿）

> “I’m currently working / as a **full stack developer** / at a tech firm. Basically, / my day-to-day job involves / developing useful applications / that **make everyday life easier and more convenient for people**. It can be quite fast-paced and challenging, / but **I find it deeply fulfilling**.
> 
> The main reason I **chose this path** / is quite simple— / I actually **majored in computer science** / back in university. Right after graduation, / I joined a tech company, / and that was where / I **crossed paths with an amazing team lead**. He noticed / that I had a **real knack for coding**, / so he brought me / into his core team.
> 
> Since then, / we’ve **shipped quite a few successful projects** together, / and even **picked up several company awards**. Looking back, / being able to develop useful applications / that really help people / gives me a **huge sense of achievement and pride**. So, / I **genuinely enjoy** what I do!”

---

### 📂 Question 1: What is your current job, or what are you studying?

**Answer:**  
“I’m currently working / as a **full stack developer** / at a tech firm. Basically, / my **day-to-day job involves** / developing useful applications / that **make everyday life easier and more convenient for people**. It can be quite **fast-paced and challenging**, / but **I find it deeply fulfilling**.”

#### 🔑 7.0+ 核心词块与考官给分点：
* `full stack developer`：全栈开发工程师（地道、精准的职业身份称谓，完胜空洞抽象的 engineer）。
* `day-to-day job involves...`：我的日常工作主要涉及……（7分标准自然口语表达，避免中式生硬直译 my job is）。
* `make everyday life easier and more convenient for people`：让人们的日常生活变得更轻松便捷（`convenient` 规范修饰生活/事物，杜绝中式直译 make people convenient）。
* `fast-paced and challenging`：节奏紧凑且富有挑战性（互联网高频地道搭配，展现真实职场状态）。
* `deeply fulfilling`：非常充实、极具成就感（7.5分高级形容词，与 Q3 的 a huge sense of achievement 形成天然的**词汇多样性梯度**，杜绝重复套话）。

---

### 📂 Question 2: Why did you choose this subject or job?

**Answer:**  
“The main reason I **chose this path** / is quite simple— / I actually **majored in computer science** / back in university. Right after graduation, / I joined a tech company, / and that was where / I **crossed paths with an amazing team lead**. He noticed / that I had a **real knack for coding**, / so he brought me / into his core team.”

#### 🔑 7.0+ 核心词块与考官给分点：
* `the main reason I chose this path`：我选择这条路的主要原因（`chose this path` 比 `chose this job` 更有职业规划质感；注意过去式 `chose` /tʃoʊz/ 的准确时态）。
* `majored in computer science`：主修计算机科学（规范地道学科搭配）。
* `crossed paths with an amazing team lead`：与一位极棒的团队主管不期而遇 / 结识（7.5分神仙动词短语，比普通 I met 更生动地道）。
* `have a real knack for coding`：在编程上很有天赋/巧门（考官最爱的地道口语短语，展现语言灵动性）。
* `brought me into his core team`：把我带入了他的核心战队（展现真实职场经历与被认可度）。

---

### 📂 Question 3: Do you enjoy what you are currently doing?

**Answer:**  
“Yes, absolutely! / I **genuinely enjoy** what I do. Since joining the team, / we’ve **shipped quite a few successful projects** together, / and even **picked up several company awards**. Looking back, / being able to develop useful applications / that really help people / gives me a **huge sense of achievement and pride**.”

#### 🔑 7.0+ 核心词块与考官给分点：
* `genuinely enjoy what I do`：由衷热爱我现在做的事（高级副词 `genuinely` 替换普通 very，表意真挚有力）。
* `since joining the team`：自加入团队以来（规范的时间连词状语）。
* `ship successful projects`：成功交付/上线优质项目（互联网行业内行高频动词 `ship`，展现行业深度，远优于 finish）。
* `picked up company awards`：斩获多项公司表彰（动词短语 `pick up` 比生硬的书面词 win 更地道口语化）。
* `a huge sense of achievement and pride`：强烈的成就感与自豪感（双重积极情感加深给分点）。

---

### 📂 Question 4: Do you plan to change your job or field in the near future?

**Answer:**  
“Definitely not! / **In the foreseeable future**, / I have **no intention of switching jobs** / or leaving this field. The main reason is that / full stack development **has so much depth to it**, / and my skills still have **plenty of room for growth**, / especially when **tackling complex engineering problems**. Right now, / I plan to stay in my current team, / **sharpen my coding skills**, / and take things **one step at a time**.”

#### 🔑 7.0+ 核心词块与考官给分点：
* `in the foreseeable future`：在可预见的未来里（7.5分高级时间状语，完美回答 future 题型）。
* `have no intention of switching jobs`：完全没有跳槽/换工作的打算（高阶意图表达）。
* `has so much depth to it`：这里面的学问非常深厚（地道英语思维短语，代替抽象难词）。
* `plenty of room for growth`：有巨大的成长空间（展现谦逊好学与成熟的职场自省力）。
* `tackling complex engineering problems`：攻克复杂的工程技术难题（动名词搭配 `tackle problems` 比 solve/do 更具专业感）。
* `sharpen my coding skills`：磨练/精进我的编程技能（生动形象的动宾搭配）。
* `take things one step at a time`：一步一个脚印，稳扎稳打（考官最爱的地道口语成语，收尾有力）。
