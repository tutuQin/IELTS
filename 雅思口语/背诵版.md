# 雅思口语 Part 1 · 核心背诵版（Work or Study）

> 💡 **母题心法（一鱼四吃）**：  
> 下方 Q1 到 Q3 全部由你的 **45 秒超级母题（Full Stack Developer）** 逐句精准无缝拆解而来！你只要背熟这一整段母题故事，就等于同时搞定了前三个问题；Q4 则顺承母题中的“团队、技术深度与成长哲学”。

---

### 🌟 超级母题全景（完整合并版 · 45秒流利说）
> “I’m currently working as a full stack developer.  
> 
> The main reason I chose this path is quite simple—I actually majored in computer science back in university. Right after graduation, I joined a tech company, and that was where I crossed paths with an amazing team lead. He noticed that I had a real knack for coding, so he brought me into his core team.  
> 
> Since then, we’ve shipped quite a few successful projects together, and even picked up several company awards. Looking back, being able to develop useful applications that make everyday life easier and more convenient for people really gives me a huge sense of achievement and pride. So, I genuinely enjoy what I do!”

---

### 📂 Question 1: What is your current job, or what are you studying?
**Answer:**  
I’m currently working as a full stack developer at a tech firm. Basically, my day-to-day job involves developing useful applications that make everyday life easier and more convenient for people. It can be quite fast-paced and challenging, but I find it deeply fulfilling.

---

### Question 2: Why did you choose this subject or job?
**Answer:**  
The main reason I chose this path is quite simple—I actually majored in computer science back in university. Right after graduation, I joined a tech company, and that was where I crossed paths with an amazing team lead. He noticed that I had a real knack for coding, so he brought me into his core team.

---

### Question 3: Do you enjoy what you are currently doing?
**Answer:**  
Yes, absolutely! I genuinely enjoy what I do. Since joining the team, we’ve shipped quite a few successful projects together, and even picked up several company awards. Looking back, being able to develop useful applications that really help people gives me a huge sense of achievement and pride.

---

### Question 4: Do you plan to change your job or field in the near future?
**Answer:**  
Definitely not! In the foreseeable future, I have no intention of switching jobs or leaving this field. The main reason is that full stack development has so much depth to it, and my skills still have plenty of room for growth, especially when tackling complex engineering problems. Right now, I plan to stay in my current team, sharpen my coding skills, and take things one step at a time.
