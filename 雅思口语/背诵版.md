# 雅思口语 Part 1 · 核心背诵版（Work or Study）

### Question 1: What is your current job, or what are you studying?
**Answer:**  
I’m currently working as a front-end developer at a tech firm. Basically, my day-to-day job involves turning lines of code into interactive and user-friendly web applications. It can be quite fast-paced and challenging at times, but I find it deeply fulfilling.

---

### Question 2: Why did you choose this subject or job?
**Answer:**  
The main reason I chose this path is quite simple—I actually majored in computer science back in university. Right after graduation, I joined a tech company, and that was where I crossed paths with an amazing team lead. He noticed that I had a real knack for coding, so he brought me into his core team. That was truly the turning point that solidified my passion for this field.

---

### Question 3: Do you enjoy what you are currently doing?
**Answer:**  
Yes, absolutely! I genuinely enjoy what I do. The most rewarding aspect is being able to develop useful applications that make everyday life easier and more convenient for people. Plus, I get to work alongside a deeply supportive team lead. Whenever we ship a successful project together, it gives me a huge sense of achievement and pride.

---

### Question 4: Do you plan to change your job or field in the near future?
**Answer:**  
Definitely not! In the foreseeable future, I have no intention of switching jobs or leaving this field. The main reason is that front-end development is evolving at a rapid pace, and there is so much depth to it. To be completely honest, my technical skills still have plenty of room for growth, especially when it comes to tackling complex engineering problems. Right now, my top priority is to master cutting-edge technologies and sharpen my coding skills. I plan to stay in my current team for at least three to four years to build a solid foundation. I truly believe that taking things one step at a time is the best way to grow.
